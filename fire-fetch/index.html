    Questions

    Is Fire-Fetch free?

    Yes. There's no account, no sign-up and no limit on how many links you paste.

    Where does the file go?

    To your browser's normal downloads folder. On iPhone, open the Files app and look in Downloads.

    Do you keep my videos or links?

    No. Files stream straight to your device and nothing is stored on our side. Your recent links list lives only in your own browser and you can clear it anytime.

    Why did a link fail?

    Private posts, age-restricted videos, live streams and some region-locked content can't be fetched. Check that the link opens in a private browser window without logging in, then try again.

    Can I download a whole playlist?

    Not yet. Paste one video at a time. For photo carousels and slideshows, every item appears so you can save each one.

    What am I allowed to download?

    Only content you own, content you have permission to save, or content that's openly licensed. Respect creators and each platform's rules.
